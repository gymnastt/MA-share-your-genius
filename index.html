The Amazing Axolotl

Animals have many incredible adaptations that help them survive. One animal with an ability that almost seems like a superpower is the axolotl. Axolotls are salamanders known for their amazing ability to regenerate damaged or lost parts of their bodies. On this website, you can learn about axolotl regeneration, explore pictures, and find resources to learn even more about this unusual animal.
